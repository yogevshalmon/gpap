* `hwmcc-c50-rare`: the geomean stands on **1 instance(s)** because CMSGen scored zero on 6 of 7. Report the win count (7/7) and the zero count, not the ratio.

* `hwmcc-c50-random`: the geomean stands on **1 instance(s)** because CMSGen scored zero on 6 of 7. Report the win count (7/7) and the zero count, not the ratio.
* Recurrent-CircuitSAT (their target table): **3 of 9** runs failed (timeout or no gradient path). They are excluded, never counted as zeros.
* Recurrent-CircuitSAT (our rare pins): **3 of 9** runs failed (timeout or no gradient path). They are excluded, never counted as zeros.
* HTS (our CNFs (both encoders)): **3 of 18** runs failed (timeout or no gradient path). They are excluded, never counted as zeros.
* GPAP/CMSGen ratios are **host-dependent**: CMSGen is single-core, and this host is 0.41x the reference laptop (verified independently by CMSGen's own propagation and decision counters). Always quote the CPU alongside the ratio.
* Sequential geomeans **exclude** b02/b05/b07/s526, whose last-cycle outputs are constant at T=25 so pinning them is a tautology; including them inflates the rare column by up to 1.9x.
* Rare-target rival ratios rest on near-zero denominators and a single rival run against GPAP's 3-seed median: read them as orders of magnitude, not constants.
